Short answer: Real-time tracking for customers means the buyer can open the order and see the current delivery status and carrier tracking link. On Shop-E-Business Tier-2 ($18/year) the Cloudflare Worker reports payment status, delivery status and the EasyPost tracking URL back to the store, where they appear on the buyer's tracking page and in Custom Order Management.

How status flows back

  1. A paid Stripe order is booked with EasyPost.
  2. The Worker sends delivery status and the tracking URL back to Shop-E-Business, signed with your shop's secret.
  3. The buyer's tracking page and your dashboard show the update.
  4. Clicking the tracking link opens the carrier's live scan history via EasyPost.

EasyPost's tracking model is described in the EasyPost Trackers API.

Why it is secure

Status updates must be signed by your shop with the shared secret, so nobody else can change an order's status. Keys stay in your own Worker (Cloudflare Workers docs).

Requirements

  • Tier-2 ($18/year) or Tier-1 plus the $8/year custom-domain add-on.
  • A verified custom domain with DNS on Cloudflare (proxied records, SSL Full (Strict)).
  • Your own Stripe account and EasyPost account with API keys.
  • Courier settings saved: sender name, ship-from address, parcel length/width/height (inches) and weight (ounces).

Stripe events used: Stripe webhooks docs.

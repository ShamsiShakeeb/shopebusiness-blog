Short answer: You can run an online store without a payment gateway by using cash on delivery. On Shop-E-Business, COD is the default checkout, so you can sell from day one for $10 per year with no merchant account or card setup. When you are ready, Tier-2 ($18/year) adds Stripe Checkout on a domain you own.

Why start without a gateway

  • No merchant account approval or paperwork.
  • Works where Stripe isn't available (Stripe global availability).
  • Buyers who don't trust online payment can still order.

Setup

  1. Sign up at shopebusiness.com and open Shop Info to add your name, logo and cover photo.
  2. Upload products and set Product Currency to the currency your buyers use.
  3. Add Delivery Charge rows per zone (for example city, rest of country, international), each with its own charge and currency.
  4. Share your store or /buy/{slug} links on Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and WhatsApp.
  5. Confirm each COD order by phone, then move it Initial → Confirmed → Packed → Shipped → Delivered.

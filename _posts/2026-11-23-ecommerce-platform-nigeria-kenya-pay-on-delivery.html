Setup

  1. Sign up at shopebusiness.com and open Shop Info to add your name, logo and cover photo.
  2. Upload products and set Product Currency to the currency your buyers use.
  3. Add Delivery Charge rows per zone (for example city, rest of country, international), each with its own charge and currency.
  4. Share your store or /buy/{slug} links on Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and WhatsApp.
  5. Confirm each COD order by phone, then move it Initial → Confirmed → Packed → Shipped → Delivered.

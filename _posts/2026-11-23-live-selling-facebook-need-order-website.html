Short answer: During a Facebook live, viewers comment "mine" faster than you can reply. An order website lets them check out themselves: share each product's /buy/ link in the live comments, and orders arrive in your dashboard with name, phone, address and delivery option. Shop-E-Business provides this for $10 per year with cash on delivery and a 0% platform fee.

Before the live

  1. Upload every item you'll show, with price and stock.
  2. Set readable product slugs so links are easy to type.
  3. Prepare a list of /buy/ links in show order.

During the live

  • Post the item's link as you present it.
  • Pin your store link at the top of the comments.
  • Ask viewers to order through the link so stock updates.

After the live

Every order lands in Orders with the buyer's name, phone and address. Move it through Initial → Confirmed → Packed → Shipped → Delivered (or Cancelled), and filter by status to see what still needs action.

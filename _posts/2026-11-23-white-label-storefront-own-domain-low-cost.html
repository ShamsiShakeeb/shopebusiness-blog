For agencies and developers

The Expose API and your own Cloudflare Worker (Cloudflare Workers docs) let developers extend the store and connect other systems without the platform holding your keys. See the Expose API and Cloudflare Worker guide.

Setup

  1. On Tier-2 ($18/year), or Tier-1 plus the $8/year add-on, verify a domain you own in Advance → Custom Domain.
  2. Move its DNS to Cloudflare, set the records to Proxied and SSL to Full (Strict).
  3. In Advance → Payment & Courier, deploy the generated Cloudflare Worker and add your Stripe secret key and webhook secret as Worker secrets.
  4. Register the Stripe webhook at yourdomain.com/stripe-webhook and click Activate; checkout switches from cash on delivery to Stripe Checkout.

DNS reference: Cloudflare DNS docs.

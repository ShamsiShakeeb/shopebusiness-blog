Short answer: Shopify is worth it for small sellers who need its app ecosystem, multi-channel selling or advanced inventory, and who have enough monthly sales to absorb a few hundred dollars a year in plan and app costs. For sellers with a modest catalog, mostly COD or social-media orders, a $10/year store such as Shop-E-Business usually gives better value.

When Shopify is worth it

  • You need specific apps from the Shopify App Store.
  • You sell across many channels and need inventory sync.
  • You use Shopify Payments, avoiding the extra third-party gateway fee.
  • Your margin easily covers the monthly cost.

When it is not

  • You make a handful of sales a week.
  • Most buyers pay cash on delivery.
  • You mainly sell through Facebook, Instagram or WhatsApp and need a checkout link.

A simple break-even test

Divide your yearly Shopify cost (plan + apps) by your average profit per order. If the result is a large share of your yearly orders, the platform is eating your margin. Example: $600/year ÷ $6 profit per order = 100 orders just to pay for the platform. At $10/year, that becomes 2 orders.

What is EasyPost?

EasyPost is a shipping API that lets software create shipments, compare carrier rates, buy labels and track parcels across many carriers. Its core objects are documented in the EasyPost Shipments API and EasyPost Trackers API.

How the integration works

  1. The buyer pays with Stripe Checkout; the money goes to your own Stripe account.
  2. Stripe sends the checkout.session.completed webhook to your store's Cloudflare Worker.
  3. The Worker reads your current ship-from address and default parcel size from Shop-E-Business.
  4. It creates an EasyPost shipment referenced by the order ID and buys the cheapest rate that succeeds.
  5. The tracking link, payment status and delivery status appear in Custom Order Management and on the buyer's tracking page.

What you need

  • Tier-2 ($18/year) or Tier-1 plus the $8/year custom-domain add-on.
  • A verified custom domain with DNS on Cloudflare (proxied records, SSL Full (Strict)).
  • Your own Stripe account and EasyPost account with API keys.
  • Courier settings saved: sender name, ship-from address, parcel length/width/height (inches) and weight (ounces).

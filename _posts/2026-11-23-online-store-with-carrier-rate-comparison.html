Short answer: Carrier rate comparison (rate shopping) means asking several carriers for a price and choosing one. On Shop-E-Business Tier-2 ($18/year), the EasyPost shipment returns rates from every carrier enabled on your account, and the Worker buys the cheapest rate that succeeds, so each paid order ships at the lowest available working price without manual comparison.

How rate comparison works

  1. The Worker creates an EasyPost shipment with addresses and parcel (EasyPost Shipments API).
  2. EasyPost returns a list of rates from your enabled carriers.
  3. The Worker tries the cheapest rate first and moves on if a purchase fails.

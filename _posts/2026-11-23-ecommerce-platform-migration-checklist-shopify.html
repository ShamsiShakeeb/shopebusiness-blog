Short answer: An ecommerce platform migration checklist for Shopify covers five areas: products, customers, domain, URLs and checkout. Export products and customers, rebuild the catalog, point DNS, redirect important URLs, and test checkout before cancelling. On Shop-E-Business the destination costs $10/year (Tier-1) or $18/year with your own domain and Stripe (Tier-2).

The five-part move checklist

Step 1: Freeze changes and export

Pick a quiet day, stop editing products in Shopify and export products and customers. Save your theme images, logo and policy pages too.

Step 2: Rebuild on the new platform

  1. Start a 3-day free trial at shopebusiness.com.
  2. Add shop details, categories and products. Use the step-by-step user manual if you need screenshots.
  3. Choose one of the 3 themes and check the store on your phone.

Step 3: Point the domain

In Advance → Custom Domain, add a TXT verification record, an A record for @ and a CNAME for www. Stripe Checkout and EasyPost shipping also need the domain's DNS on Cloudflare with proxied records; see the Cloudflare DNS docs. The full walkthrough is in the custom domain, Stripe and courier setup guide.

Step 4: Protect your search traffic

Redirect old Shopify URLs that get traffic, as recommended in Google's redirect documentation. Then submit the new sitemap.

Step 5: Test, then cancel

Place a COD test order and, on Tier-2, a Stripe test-mode order. Only cancel Shopify after both work. Current Shopify plans are listed on Shopify pricing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I transfer a Shopify store automatically?

Shopify lets you export products and customers as CSV. On Shop-E-Business you then recreate products in the dashboard; there is no limit on product count.

Do I need to transfer my domain away from Shopify?

No. If the domain is registered elsewhere, just change its DNS records. If you bought it through Shopify, you can keep it there or transfer it to another registrar first.

What happens to my Shopify orders history?

Export it before cancelling. Shop-E-Business starts a fresh order dashboard for new orders.

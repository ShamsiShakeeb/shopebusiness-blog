Why do small sellers leave Shopify?

Shopify bills every month, and most stores add paid apps on top. If you don't use Shopify Payments, Shopify also adds a transaction fee on each order. For a store with a few dozen orders a month, the yearly bill is often several hundred dollars. Check the current numbers on Shopify pricing.

How do I migrate from Shopify step by step?

  1. Export your data. In Shopify admin, export products and customers as CSV files so you have a full record of titles, prices, descriptions and image URLs.
  2. Start the Shop-E-Business trial. Register at shopebusiness.com (3 days free, no card). Your store goes live on yourshop.shopebusiness.com.
  3. Recreate categories and products. Add each product with photos, price, discount, description and SEO fields. Products are unlimited. Reuse your Shopify product handles as slugs so URLs stay familiar.
  4. Pick a theme from the 3 included storefront themes.
  5. Choose checkout. Cash on delivery is the default. For cards, use Stripe Checkout on Tier-2; payments go straight to your own Stripe account.
  6. Move your domain. On Tier-2 (or the $8/year add-on), connect your domain with TXT, A and CNAME records.
  7. Redirect old URLs. Shopify URLs such as /products/handle will change. Follow Google's site-move guide to map important old URLs to new ones.
  8. Cancel Shopify only after test orders work on the new store.

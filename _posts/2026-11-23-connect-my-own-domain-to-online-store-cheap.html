The three DNS records

TypeNameValue
TXT_sb-challengeYour verification token
A@The platform IP shown in your dashboard
CNAMEwwwshopebusiness.com

Steps

  1. Open Advance → Custom Domain and copy the records.
  2. Add them at your DNS provider and click Verify & Save Domain.
  3. Add the domain to Cloudflare and replace nameservers at your registrar (Cloudflare DNS docs).
  4. Switch records to Proxied and set SSL to Full (Strict) (Cloudflare SSL Full (Strict)).

Why F-commerce sellers need a real website

The problems with selling only on Facebook

  • Manual order taking: "Price please?", "Inbox koren" — every sale needs a chat, name, phone and address typed by hand.

  • Trust: Customers hesitate to pay or order from a page with no website. A proper store link looks professional.
  • Platform risk: Page restrictions, reach drops and ad-account bans can stop your income instantly.

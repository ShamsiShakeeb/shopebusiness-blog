Short answer: Shipping notification emails tell buyers their parcel is on its way. On Shop-E-Business Tier-2 ($18/year), the shipment and tracking link are created automatically and appear on the buyer's tracking page; the platform's email alerts go to the seller for new orders. If you want buyer shipping emails, a developer can extend the Cloudflare Worker to send them when the label is bought.

Adding buyer emails

  1. In the Worker, after the EasyPost buy step, call your email provider's API.
  2. Include the tracking URL returned by EasyPost (EasyPost Trackers API).
  3. Store email API keys as Worker secrets (Cloudflare Workers docs).

Developer overview: Expose API and Cloudflare Worker guide. Stripe can also send payment receipts; see Stripe Checkout docs.

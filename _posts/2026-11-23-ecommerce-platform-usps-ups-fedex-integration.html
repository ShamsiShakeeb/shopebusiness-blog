Short answer: Shop-E-Business connects to USPS, UPS, FedEx and other carriers through EasyPost rather than separate carrier integrations. On Tier-2 ($18/year), each paid Stripe order requests rates from the carriers enabled on your EasyPost account and buys the cheapest rate that succeeds, saving the tracking link to the order.

One API, several carriers

EasyPost aggregates carriers behind one shipment API (EasyPost Shipments API). Which carriers you can use depends on what is enabled on your EasyPost account and your shipping country.

How the carrier is picked

The Worker buys the cheapest rate that succeeds. If you want a specific carrier or service level, developers can adjust the Worker logic; see the Expose API and Cloudflare Worker guide.

Parcel data sent to carriers

Default parcel length, width and height in inches and weight in ounces, plus the ship-from address and sender name (used as customs signer for international parcels).

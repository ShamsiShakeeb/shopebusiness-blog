Short answer: In many African markets buyers prefer to pay on delivery, in cash or by mobile money to the rider. Shop-E-Business supports this with a cash-on-delivery checkout by default: buyers order with name, phone and address, and you collect payment at the door however you and your courier accept it. There is no built-in mobile-money gateway; the store costs $10 per year with a 0% platform fee.

Setup

  1. Sign up at shopebusiness.com and open Shop Info to add your name, logo and cover photo.
  2. Upload products and set Product Currency to the currency your buyers use.

Checkout

The COD checkout asks only for name, phone number and delivery address, with no customer account, card or email verification. COD is the default on every plan; Tier-2 ($18/year) adds Stripe card payments on a domain you own where Stripe is available (Stripe global availability), and activating Stripe switches checkout from COD to card payment.

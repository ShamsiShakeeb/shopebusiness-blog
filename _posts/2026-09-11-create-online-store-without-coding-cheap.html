Create your online store in 5 steps (no coding)

Go to shopebusiness.com and enter your brand name. Your store is generated automatically at yourbrand.shopebusiness.com — an instant subdomain with hosting and SSL already handled.

Step 3: Choose a theme and add your branding

Step 5: Share your link and take orders

Post your store link on Instagram, TikTok, WhatsApp and Facebook. Customers order with name, phone and address (cash on delivery is built in), and every order appears in your dashboard.

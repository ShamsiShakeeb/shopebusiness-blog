The hidden cost: waiting for your money

On Lazada, cash from a sale is not yours on the day you ship. The order has to be delivered, then survive the return window, then wait for the next payout cycle. For a small seller buying stock with their own money, two to three weeks of locked cash means fewer units on the shelf.

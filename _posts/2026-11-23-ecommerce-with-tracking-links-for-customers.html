Short answer: On Shop-E-Business Tier-2 ($18/year), each paid Stripe order gets an EasyPost shipment, and its tracking link is saved automatically. The link appears in the seller's Custom Order Management screen and on the buyer's tracking page, along with payment status and delivery status, so customers can check their parcel without messaging you.

Why tracking links matter

"Where is my order?" is one of the most common support questions in ecommerce. A tracking link on the order page answers it before the buyer asks. EasyPost generates tracking through its EasyPost Trackers API.

What the buyer sees

  • Payment status (for example Paid or Refunded).
  • Delivery status reported back from the Worker.
  • The EasyPost tracking link for the parcel.

What the seller sees

In Advance → Custom Order Management the same order shows payment status, delivery status and the tracking link, so you can share it on WhatsApp or Messenger if a buyer asks.

How the link is created

  1. The buyer pays with Stripe Checkout; the money goes to your own Stripe account.
  2. Stripe sends the checkout.session.completed webhook to your store's Cloudflare Worker.
  3. The Worker reads your current ship-from address and default parcel size from Shop-E-Business.
  4. It creates an EasyPost shipment referenced by the order ID and buys the cheapest rate that succeeds.
  5. The tracking link, payment status and delivery status appear in Custom Order Management and on the buyer's tracking page.

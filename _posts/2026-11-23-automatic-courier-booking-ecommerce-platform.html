Short answer: Automatic courier booking means the store books the shipment with a courier the moment an order is paid, instead of the seller doing it by hand. Shop-E-Business Tier-2 ($18/year) does this through EasyPost: a paid Stripe order becomes a booked shipment with a purchased label and a tracking link, using your own EasyPost account.

What counts as automatic courier booking?

Booking a courier means creating a shipment record with the carrier and buying postage. In an automatic flow, software does both from the order data. EasyPost exposes this as a single shipment-and-buy flow; see the EasyPost Shipments API.

How Shop-E-Business books the courier

  1. The buyer pays with Stripe Checkout; the money goes to your own Stripe account.
  2. Stripe sends the checkout.session.completed webhook to your store's Cloudflare Worker.
  3. The Worker reads your current ship-from address and default parcel size from Shop-E-Business.
  4. It creates an EasyPost shipment referenced by the order ID and buys the cheapest rate that succeeds.
  5. The tracking link, payment status and delivery status appear in Custom Order Management and on the buyer's tracking page.

Requirements

  • Tier-2 ($18/year) or Tier-1 plus the $8/year custom-domain add-on.
  • A verified custom domain with DNS on Cloudflare (proxied records, SSL Full (Strict)).
  • Your own Stripe account and EasyPost account with API keys.
  • Courier settings saved: sender name, ship-from address, parcel length/width/height (inches) and weight (ounces).

Carriers depend on your EasyPost account and can include UPS business solutions and FedEx shipping. Developers can customize the Worker to add other couriers: Expose API and Cloudflare Worker guide.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is automatic courier booking?

The store books the shipment and buys postage with a courier automatically when an order is paid.

Which courier does Shop-E-Business book?

It books through EasyPost, using the carriers enabled on your own EasyPost account.

What happens if booking fails?

The order shows Courier error and Stripe retries the webhook; fixing the issue lets the next retry book it without a duplicate label.

Can developers add another courier?

Yes. The Cloudflare Worker can be customized to plug in other couriers or gateways.

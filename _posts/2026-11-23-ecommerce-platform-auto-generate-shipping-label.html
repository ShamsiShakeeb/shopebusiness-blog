Short answer: To auto-generate a shipping label, a platform needs three inputs (a to-address, a from-address and a parcel) and one action (buying a rate). Shop-E-Business Tier-2 ($18/year) gets the to-address from the paid Stripe order, the from-address and parcel from your saved courier settings, and buys the cheapest successful EasyPost rate, producing the label and tracking code automatically.

Triggering the label

The trigger is Stripe's checkout.session.completed webhook, delivered to your Cloudflare Worker at yourdomain.com/stripe-webhook. See Stripe webhooks docs.

Safety built in

  • Orders sent to the Worker carry a short-lived signed token.
  • Shipments are looked up by order reference before buying, so retries never create a duplicate label.
  • Outbound calls time out after 15 seconds and error messages are scrubbed of keys.

  • Tier-2 ($18/year) or Tier-1 plus the $8/year custom-domain add-on.
  • A verified custom domain with DNS on Cloudflare (proxied records, SSL Full (Strict)).
  • Your own Stripe account and EasyPost account with API keys.
  • Courier settings saved: sender name, ship-from address, parcel length/width/height (inches) and weight (ounces).

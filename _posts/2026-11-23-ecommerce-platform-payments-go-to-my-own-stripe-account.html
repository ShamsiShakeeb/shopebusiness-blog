Short answer: On Shop-E-Business, card payments go to your own Stripe account, not to a platform balance. Tier-2 ($18/year) uses your Stripe secret key, stored only in your own Cloudflare Worker, to create Stripe Checkout sessions, so Stripe settles funds and payouts directly to you on your Stripe schedule, and the platform takes 0%.

Platforms that route payments for many sellers often use Stripe Connect (Stripe Connect docs); Shop-E-Business instead uses your own Stripe keys.

Where your keys live

  • STRIPE_SECRET_KEY and STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET are encrypted Worker secrets.
  • Shop-E-Business never stores them; the Worker runs on Cloudflare (Cloudflare Workers docs).
  • Webhooks are signature-verified (Stripe webhooks docs).

Setup

  1. On Tier-2 ($18/year), or Tier-1 plus the $8/year add-on, verify a domain you own in Advance → Custom Domain.
  2. Move its DNS to Cloudflare, set the records to Proxied and SSL to Full (Strict).
  3. In Advance → Payment & Courier, deploy the generated Cloudflare Worker and add your Stripe secret key and webhook secret as Worker secrets.
  4. Register the Stripe webhook at yourdomain.com/stripe-webhook and click Activate; checkout switches from cash on delivery to Stripe Checkout.

Which should you choose?

Choose Shopify Basic if

  • You need many third-party apps or multi-channel inventory sync.
  • You already rely on Shopify Payments.

Choose a yearly flat-fee builder if

  • You sell mainly by COD or through Facebook, Instagram or WhatsApp.
  • You want one predictable bill a year and 0% platform fees.

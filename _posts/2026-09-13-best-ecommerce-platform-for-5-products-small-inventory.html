What a small-inventory seller actually needs

The short list

  • A clean product page with photos, price, variants (size/colour) and stock.
  • A checkout a customer can finish in one tap on a phone.
  • An order list you can manage from your phone.
  • Pages Google can index, so your products are found.
  • A price that does not exceed your monthly profit.

Everything else — abandoned-cart AI, multi-warehouse routing, B2B price lists — is noise for a 5–20 product store.

Behind the scenes

  1. The buyer pays with Stripe Checkout; the money goes to your own Stripe account.
  2. Stripe sends the checkout.session.completed webhook to your store's Cloudflare Worker.
  3. The Worker reads your current ship-from address and default parcel size from Shop-E-Business.
  4. It creates an EasyPost shipment referenced by the order ID and buys the cheapest rate that succeeds.
  5. The tracking link, payment status and delivery status appear in Custom Order Management and on the buyer's tracking page.

References: EasyPost Shipments API, Stripe webhooks docs, Cloudflare Workers docs.

1. Cost: what a full year really costs

Shopify and Wix charge a subscription every month, so a small store typically pays several hundred dollars per year before apps. WooCommerce has no subscription, but hosting, a domain, SSL and premium plugins add up — and so does the time you spend on updates. Shop-E-Business charges one flat fee per year and takes 0% of your sales. At $10 a year, a single order usually pays for the whole year.

2. Checkout: card-first vs COD-first

Shopify, Wix and WooCommerce were built around online card payments, with cash on delivery as a manual option. Shop-E-Business is built the other way around: customers enter only name, phone and address, no account is required, and no payment gateway is needed. In COD-heavy markets like Bangladesh, India, Pakistan and Thailand, that shorter checkout means fewer abandoned orders.

3. Setup and maintenance

Shop-E-Business, Shopify and Wix are hosted — sign up and add products. WooCommerce gives full control but you manage hosting, WordPress, plugins, backups and security. Shop-E-Business is the quickest to launch because it focuses on what social sellers need: product pages, shareable Order Now links, COD checkout, an order dashboard, email alerts and SEO basics.

4. Which one should you pick?

  • Shop-E-Business — you sell through Facebook, Instagram, TikTok or WhatsApp, customers pay on delivery, and you want a professional store at the lowest predictable cost.
  • Shopify — you are scaling a card-first brand where Shopify Payments is available and you need a big app marketplace.
  • WooCommerce — you already run WordPress and can maintain it.
  • Wix — design-heavy website first, shop second.

Why cash on delivery is the right default in Bangladesh

Cash on delivery is not a "fallback" in Bangladesh — it is how the market works. Most shoppers prefer to see the product before paying, many don't use cards, and trust in new online shops is still being built. A store that forces online payment simply loses orders.

What COD gives a new seller

  • Higher conversion: "Pay when you receive" removes the biggest hesitation for first-time buyers.
  • No gateway fees: Gateways charge 1.5–3% plus setup and monthly costs. COD costs 0%.
  • No approval process: No bank account verification, trade license or merchant agreement needed to start.
  • Works with every courier: Steadfast, Pathao, RedX, Paperfly, Sundarban — all collect cash and remit to you.

The COD-first workflow in Shop-E-Business

1. Customer orders in one tap

On any product page the customer adds items to the cart and enters just three things: name, phone number and delivery address. No account creation, no OTP, no card form. The order appears instantly in your admin dashboard with status Initial.

2. You confirm by phone

Call or WhatsApp the customer to confirm the order and address, then mark it Confirmed. This one step cuts fake orders and return rates dramatically — a best practice every experienced COD seller follows.

3. Hand to courier, collect cash

Pack the parcel, book it with your courier and mark the order Shipped. The courier collects the product price plus delivery charge from the customer and pays you out. Mark Delivered once it is done. Every status change is visible in your order list.

4. Track everything from your phone

Filter orders by status, see daily sales and manage stock from the mobile-friendly admin panel. With COD there is no gateway to reconcile — what the courier remits is your revenue.

Tips to reduce COD returns

  1. Always confirm the order by phone before shipping.

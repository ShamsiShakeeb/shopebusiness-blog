Why COD matters in the region

Payment-method shares by country are tracked in the Worldpay Global Payments Report; cash and cash on delivery still feature in many Middle East markets alongside cards and wallets. Account ownership data is in the World Bank Global Findex.

The COD checkout asks only for name, phone number and delivery address, with no customer account, card or email verification.

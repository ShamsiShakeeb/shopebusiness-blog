Why WooCommerce hosting gets expensive

  • Performance: stores need more PHP memory and database capacity than a blog, so cheap shared plans slow down.
  • Renewal pricing: many hosts discount the first term and renew at a higher rate.
  • Add-ons: backups, CDN, malware scanning and staging are often extra.

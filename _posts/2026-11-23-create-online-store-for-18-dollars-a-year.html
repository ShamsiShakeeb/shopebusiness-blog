How do I set up my $18-a-year store?

Step 1: Build the store on the free trial

  1. Start the 3-day free trial (no card).
  2. Add shop details, categories and unlimited products, and pick one of the 3 themes.
  3. Buy a Tier-2 license key and paste it into the dashboard. The custom domain is not available during the trial.

Step 2: Connect your custom domain

  1. Open Advance → Custom Domain.
  2. Add three DNS records: TXT _sb-challenge = your token, A @ = the server IP shown in the tab, CNAME www = shopebusiness.com.
  3. Type your root domain and click Verify & Save Domain. Your old subdomain link then redirects to the new domain.
  4. On Cloudflare, switch the A and CNAME records to Proxied and set SSL to Full (Strict).

Step 3: Turn on Stripe Checkout and EasyPost shipping

  1. Open Advance → Payment & Courier and save your ship-from address and default parcel size.
  2. Copy the generated Cloudflare Worker script, deploy it and add the routes yourdomain.com/apiEndpoint and yourdomain.com/stripe-webhook.
  3. Add your Stripe and EasyPost keys as Worker secrets, register the Stripe webhook and click Activate.

Buyers then pay on Stripe Checkout, and every paid order is booked as an EasyPost shipment with a tracking link that shows in your order dashboard. Your keys stay in your own Worker. Full walkthrough with every field: custom domain, Stripe and courier setup, step by step.

Short answer: To move your Shopify store to a cheaper platform, start with the catalog: export products from Products → Export as CSV, download product images, then rebuild each listing on the new store. The columns that matter are Handle, Title, Body (HTML), Variant Price, Variant Compare At Price, Image Src, SEO Title and SEO Description. On Shop-E-Business those map to slug, title, description, price, discount, images and meta tags, for $10/year.

Which Shopify CSV columns should I keep?

How do I rebuild the products?

  1. Open the CSV in a spreadsheet and sort by Handle.
  2. Download each image from the Image Src URL before you close Shopify.
  3. In your Shop-E-Business dashboard, create categories first, then add each product with its photos, price, discount, description and SEO fields.
  4. Keep meta titles short and descriptive; the product SEO and slug guide explains how slugs and meta tags work.

Does structured data carry over?

Search engines read product details from page markup such as schema.org Product. When you rebuild a product, write a clear title, price and description so the new page is as complete as the old one. Google's Google's site-move guide covers the rest of a platform move.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is the Shopify product export?

In Shopify admin go to Products and click Export, then choose CSV. Shopify emails or downloads the file.

Do Shopify image URLs keep working after I cancel?

Do not rely on it. Download every image from the Image Src column before you close the Shopify store.

Can I keep my product URLs?

You can reuse the Shopify handle as the product slug, but the path before it changes, so redirect important old URLs if you keep the same domain.

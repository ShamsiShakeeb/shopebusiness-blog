Short answer: Automatic shipment booking means each paid order is booked with a carrier without the seller opening a shipping site. A Shop-E-Business Tier-2 store ($18/year) books an EasyPost shipment for every paid Stripe order, buys the label and records the tracking link, then reports payment and delivery status back to your dashboard.

The booking flow in one table

StageWhat happens
Order placedBuyer pays on Stripe Checkout
WebhookStripe notifies your Worker
BookingWorker creates the EasyPost shipment and buys a rate
StatusTracking link, payment and delivery status sent back to the store

Docs: Stripe Checkout docs, EasyPost Shipments API, Cloudflare Workers docs.

Retries without double-booking

The Worker returns success to Stripe only after every step works. If booking fails, Stripe retries for up to about three days; because shipments are looked up by order reference first, the retry never books twice.

Turning it on

  1. Verify your custom domain on Tier-2 (or the $8/year add-on) and move DNS to Cloudflare.
  2. Save courier settings in Advance → Payment & Courier.
  3. Deploy the generated Worker and add your Stripe and EasyPost secrets.
  4. Register the Stripe webhook and click Activate.

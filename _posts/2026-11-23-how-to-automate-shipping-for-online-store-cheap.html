Short answer: The cheapest way to automate shipping for an online store is to connect your store's payment events directly to a shipping API, instead of paying for a separate shipping app. On Shop-E-Business Tier-2 ($18/year) you save courier settings, deploy the generated Cloudflare Worker with your Stripe and EasyPost keys, register the Stripe webhook and click Activate.

Step-by-step

  1. Get Tier-2 ($18/year) or add the $8/year custom-domain add-on to Tier-1.
  2. Verify your domain in Advance → Custom Domain and move DNS to Cloudflare with proxied records (Cloudflare DNS docs).
  3. Save courier settings: sender name, ship-from address, default parcel size and weight.
  4. Create an EasyPost account and copy the test API key.
  5. Deploy the Worker script shown in Payment & Courier to Cloudflare Workers (Cloudflare Workers docs), add the routes and secrets.
  6. Register the Stripe webhook at yourdomain.com/stripe-webhook (Stripe webhooks docs).
  7. Activate, place a test order with card 4242… and confirm a tracking link appears.
  8. Switch to live keys and redeploy.

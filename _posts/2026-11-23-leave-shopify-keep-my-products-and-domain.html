Cancel Shopify only after test orders work.

Before you cancel: export everything

  • Products CSV (titles, prices, descriptions, image URLs).
  • Customers CSV and orders CSV for your records.
  • Product photos, logo and policy text.
  • Any open orders that still need fulfilment.

Also note any paid apps; uninstalling them stops their separate billing. Check Shopify pricing and your billing page for what remains due.

  1. Start the free trial at shopebusiness.com. Your store is live on a free subdomain right away.
  2. Add products (unlimited) and pick one of the 3 themes.
  3. Keep COD as the default checkout, or move to Tier-2 for Stripe Checkout.
  4. Place a test order and check the email alert.

Switch traffic, then cancel

  1. Point your domain to the new store (Tier-2 or $8/year add-on). The custom domain, Stripe and courier setup guide has the exact DNS records.
  2. Update your Facebook page, Instagram bio, WhatsApp catalog link and ads.
  3. Redirect important URLs using Google's redirect documentation.
  4. Fulfil remaining Shopify orders, then close the Shopify store.

Where labels come from

Labels are bought through EasyPost from the carriers enabled on your EasyPost account, such as USPS business shipping. EasyPost describes shipment creation in the EasyPost Shipments API and package dimensions in the EasyPost Parcels API.

Courier settings you save once

  • Sender name (also used as customs signer for international parcels).
  • Ship-from street, city, postal code and country.
  • Default parcel length, width, height (inches) and weight (ounces).

The Worker reads these at shipment time, so edits need no redeploy.

Failures and refunds

If the label can't be bought, the order shows Courier error and Stripe retries the webhook; the next retry books the shipment without buying a second label. A Stripe refund triggers a best-effort label refund. Platform comparison: Shop-E-Business vs Shopify vs WooCommerce vs Wix.

Short answer: Shopify is often too expensive for a small store because the costs stack: a monthly plan, paid apps, and an extra transaction fee if you don't use Shopify Payments. Where does the Shopify bill come from?

  1. Plan fee: charged monthly or yearly; see Shopify pricing.
  2. Apps: reviews, upsells, COD forms and SEO tools on the Shopify App Store are often $5–30/month each.
  3. Transaction fees: Shopify adds a platform fee on each order if you use a third-party payment gateway.
  4. Card processing: charged by whichever processor you use, for example Stripe pricing.

When is Shopify still worth it?

Shopify makes sense for brands that need hundreds of integrations, multi-location inventory or a large app ecosystem. If you sell a few dozen to a few hundred products, mostly by COD or through social media, you are likely paying for features you don't use.

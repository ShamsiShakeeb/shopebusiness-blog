Short answer: Shop-E-Business sends an email alert for every new order and collects all orders in one dashboard. Each order shows product, size, quantity, customer details and totals, and moves through Initial, Confirmed, Packed, Shipped and Delivered statuses. This is included on the $10 per year Tier-1 plan.

What the dashboard shows

The Dashboard shows live counters for Orders, Products, Deliverable Orders and New Orders. Each order in Orders lists product, size, quantity, customer name, phone, address, note and an items + shipping = total summary, and new orders trigger an email alert.

Daily routine

  1. Read the email alert.
  2. Open Orders, filter by Initial.
  3. Confirm by phone or WhatsApp (WhatsApp Business).
  4. Update to Packed, Shipped, Delivered.
